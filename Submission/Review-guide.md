# App Review preparation and deterministic media

This is a preparation guide, not an assertion that a review build is ready. No review account is needed for the app itself. Normal external websites may require their own login, which the browser does not bypass. All production features, including source discovery and saving, must be disclosed identically to review and customers.

Use only original CC0 calibration content in review examples and screenshots. The app does not include or curate adult content. The generated clips in Fixtures/generated have a documented licence in Fixtures/README.md and LICENSE.txt. No private downloads, signed site URLs or account data belong in submission materials.

The preparation script stages a calibration page and two small original CC0 MP4s in `Submission/Prepared/site/`. Before submission, host this subset of these fixtures at a durable public HTTPS address under the publisher's control, with permission to stream/save and correct MP4 range/MIME handling and HLS MIME types. Do not give Apple a Mac-only loopback address. Alternatively supply the original MP4 as a review attachment with clear Files/import instructions and separately provide a public browser/playback test page. Public hosting and its URL are still pending; do not invent a working address.

Suggested acceptance journey after real public URLs are configured:

1. Open the supplied calibration test page using the address control. Start its video and choose Watch in VR.
2. Confirm automatic VR180/Side by side on the labelled SBS clip, changing to Screen/Mono for the ordinary clip. Check pause, resume, seek, recenter, quality and Return to page.
3. Save the permitted MP4, add a second permitted clip while saving, open Downloads and verify the saved copy without network access. Import the provided MP4 through Files; export/delete a saved copy.
4. Open Browser settings → Privacy policy and Third-party licences. Fresh installs have detailed history off. Enable, perform a playback action, inspect Playback details, then delete diagnostic history and verify saved media remains.
5. Review normal browsing, back/forward/reload, bookmarks and restoration choices. Clear website data signs out while preserving intentional bookmarks and saved media.

Capture real app screenshots with only the original CC0 clips and neutral browsing pages. Simulator scene integration can prepare mounted screens; it does not replace gaze/pinch input and stereo comfort checks on a physical Vision Pro. No final App Store screenshots or publicly reachable review fixture URL are asserted by this guide.

Keep source authorization documents, export classification, publisher/support details, App Store age/privacy questionnaire, LGPL source delivery and hardware acceptance linked to the specific signed build before upload. The local archive helper never submits to Apple.
