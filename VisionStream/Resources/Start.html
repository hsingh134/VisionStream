VisionStream

Search or enter a website above. Use ＋ for a new tab, and select a tab to return to its page.

Keep your favourite pages close. Select the star beside the address to add a favourite. Favourites appear above Home and in the address box. The bookmark button lets you find and manage all saved pages.

Watch a video. Press the website’s Play button to open compatible video in the native viewer. If it stays on the webpage, use Watch in VR when a compatible source is detected.

Adjust the picture. Format detects VR180/VR360 and stereo layout automatically. If the image looks doubled, choose Side by side or Top / bottom. Recenter and Position let you move the view, including while reclining.

Save for later. Video tools can find sources or save supported video. Downloads holds saved copies for offline playback. Save only content the source permits you to download.

Hold Back or Forward for recent pages in this tab. With a keyboard, use ⌘L for the address box, ⌘T for a new tab and ⌘W to close it.

Tabs share website logins and stay open only while the app runs. Background tabs have media suspended; their websites can still make network requests. Browser settings controls startup restoration and optional troubleshooting history. Clear website data signs you out across all tabs.

Some websites require their own player or login. A website’s motion/VR setup affects its own player and does not configure VisionStream.
