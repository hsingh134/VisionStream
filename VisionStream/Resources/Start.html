VisionStream

Enter a website address above, then press the website’s Play button. Compatible videos open in VisionStream’s native VR viewer automatically. Turn this off in Browser settings if you prefer to stay on the webpage.

Automatic format uses supported video metadata, clear labels and repeated image comparisons. Ambiguous videos start on a flat screen. Open Video format before or during playback to adjust it.

If you see two images next to each other, choose Side by side. For stacked images, choose Top / bottom. Choose VR180 or VR360 separately to match the video.

The video bar appears only when a compatible source is detected. If a website’s Play control is not recognised, use Watch in VR. Video tools can scan again, find more sources, save a compatible video, or open a playback report. While one saves, choose Add to queue to add another.

Downloads holds videos saved on this headset. You can play them offline or delete them to free storage. Keep VisionStream open for MP4 saves and supported HLS-to-MP4 recovery. Native HLS packages use Apple’s background service. Waiting saves run one at a time and clear when the app quits. Some HLS formats cannot be saved.

The ••• menu always provides source finding and playback details, opens video links and VR libraries, explains the controls, and lets you clear website data. Clearing website data signs you out; downloaded videos stay on the headset.

Some websites require their own player or login. A website’s motion/VR setup affects its own player and does not configure VisionStream.

Closing an immersive video returns you to the webpage. Cookies and website storage persist on this device. Browser settings contains the privacy policy, optional troubleshooting history and diagnostic deletion controls. Save only content the source permits you to download.
